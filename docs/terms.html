3. What WhatPort Pro is

WhatPort Pro unlocks the Flight Recorder inside the same WhatPort app. It is not a separate download. It requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, and runs on both Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.

On Intel Macs the app runs in reduced detail, because they do not publish the USB-C port controller data Apple Silicon does. The Flight Recorder works, but only on the data that machine reports: per-port USB-PD contracts, pin configuration and port health counters are not available on Intel and so are absent from recordings and exports made there.

11. What WhatPort is, and is not

WhatPort reads the information macOS exposes about your ports, links, power contracts and connected devices, and explains it in plain English. It works from what the hardware reports rather than by physically measuring anything, which makes it a diagnostic tool rather than a test instrument.

Some of what WhatPort shows comes from data a device, dock, charger or cable reports about itself. Any of those can misreport, whether through a manufacturing fault, a design shortcut or deliberate misrepresentation. WhatPort can only tell you what was reported and what the connection actually achieved.

It follows that:

  • WhatPort output is not a certification. It is not a USB Implementers Forum or Thunderbolt certification, a conformance or compliance test, or proof that a port, cable or device is genuine, counterfeit, safe or unsafe. Do not present it as any of those, to a supplier, a customer or anyone else.
  • Port health scoring is not a hardware diagnosis. It counts how often faults recur on a port over time. A poor score is a prompt to investigate, not a finding that the hardware is defective, and it is not evidence for a warranty or repair claim on its own.
  • Findings describe data, not manufacturers. Where WhatPort flags a reading as unusual or inconsistent, that is a statement about the data read from that specific unit on that specific connection. It is not an assertion about any named manufacturer, brand or product line, and must not be represented as one.
  • Do not rely on it alone for consequential decisions. WhatPort must not be the sole basis for any decision where being wrong could cause injury, property damage or financial loss. Verify independently.
  • Not for safety-critical use. WhatPort is not designed, tested or certified for use in medical, aviation, automotive, maritime, industrial control, nuclear or any other environment where failure could lead to death, injury or serious harm. Do not use it in those settings.
