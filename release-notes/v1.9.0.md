Your ports keep their own service history. Now you can read it.

Every USB-C port controller counts its faults in firmware: hard resets, failed role swaps, short detections, bus errors. The counts survive reboots and sit unread on every Apple Silicon MacBook. WhatPort now reads them.

## New

- **PD reliability counters in Port Health (Pro).** The Health tab now folds the port controller's own lifetime fault counters into each port's score: PD hard resets, role-swap failures, short-detect events, and I2C errors. A port that keeps renegotiating its power contract now shows up as unhealthy even when the USB side looks clean.

- **Only new faults count.** The first time this version sees your ports it quietly records the existing counts as the starting point, so nothing you did before installing WhatPort is held against your score. Reset Health Counters draws a fresh line whenever you want one.

## Under the hood

- The counters are attributed to ports using a rule validated against hundreds of real Macs and cross-checked at runtime against the SMC's own per-port identifiers. If the checks ever disagree on a machine, WhatPort shows nothing for that machine rather than guessing, and the fault counters simply sit out.

## Install

Download below, unzip, and drag WhatPort.app to Applications, or:

```
brew upgrade --cask whatport
```

The app is signed and notarised by Apple.
