Charging power that was missing

Four power fixes, most of them on Macs that were showing nothing at all.

## Fixed

- **M1 Pro, M1 Max and M1 Ultra now show USB-C charging power.** macOS never publishes the charging contract for a USB-C port on those machines, so WhatPort had nothing to read: the port showed as connected with no power while the Mac pulled 100 W. The contract is in the SMC all along, and WhatPort now reads it there. It stays quiet whenever macOS does answer, so nothing changes on the Macs that were already right.

- **MagSafe keeps its wattage when the battery is full.** Power only appeared while the battery was actively charging, so sitting at 100% (or paused at 80% by optimised charging) blanked the port. The charger is still carrying the machine's running load, and the port now says so.

- **A slow charge over USB-C is no longer ignored.** A 5 V supply, like a phone charger or a bus-powered hub, is reported by some Macs a fraction under 5 V, and WhatPort was rejecting it as a bad reading. Those ports showed no power at all while charging at 15 W.

- **Lifetime port statistics work on an empty port.** They are lifetime counters, so an idle port is exactly when you want to read them, and they were only available while something was plugged in. Worse, on a port with a dock attached, the numbers could come from the dock's own ports rather than the Mac's.

## Also

- The port list no longer depends on WhatPort recognising a list of chip-specific names, which is the kind of thing that quietly stops working on new hardware. Every port on an unfamiliar Mac would have lost its health counters, liquid detection and transport list, with nothing looking broken.

- The IOKit readers are now tested against 787 real Macs from the WhatCable probe corpus, spanning M1 through M5, rather than only against whichever Mac happens to be running the tests. That is how the 5 V charging bug above was found: nobody reported it.

## One thing you may notice

If you have used "Reset Health Counters" before, a port may show a small number of old faults once. The counters it was measured against were wrong, and the fixes above corrected them. Reset it again and it will stay clear.

## Install

Download below, unzip, and drag WhatPort.app to Applications, or:

```
brew upgrade --cask whatport
```

The app is signed and notarised by Apple.
